Welcome to North Star Bakery

North Star Bakery has been baking fresh bread, pastries, and cakes for our neighborhood since 2013. Every loaf is shaped by hand and baked in small batches, so it always tastes like home.

What We're Known For

Our sourdough, cinnamon rolls, and custom celebration cakes are neighborhood favorites, made with locally sourced flour and traditional techniques passed down through three generations of bakers.

Hours & Location

Open Tuesday through Sunday, 7:00 AM to 3:00 PM, at 118 North Star Avenue. Closed Mondays so our team can rest and prep for the week ahead.
