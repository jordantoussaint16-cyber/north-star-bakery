Our Story

North Star Bakery began at a family kitchen table and has grown into a beloved neighborhood gathering place, serving commuters, families, and local event planners for more than a decade.

Sourcing Philosophy

We partner with regional mills and local farms to source organic flour, seasonal fruit, and dairy, keeping our ingredients close to home and our community connected to where its food comes from.

Staff Highlights

Our head baker brings over 20 years of experience in traditional sourdough fermentation, and our pastry team trained locally before joining North Star Bakery.
